# Creatives and copy for Meta campaigns

## Picking the best existing ads

- **Criterion**: return (ROAS or revenue), conversion volume, or cost per conversion. Ask
  which matters; without revenue tracking, use conversions then cost per conversion.
- **Window**: last 7, 30 or 90 days (90 is the catalog default; it varies per project).
- **Consolidate by creative**: the same creative running in several ad sets counts once,
  with its spend and results summed, so a winner is not judged in pieces.
- Ignore ads with too little spend to judge.

## Creative mix

- Andromeda rewards **diversity**: different formats (video, static, carousel) and
  different copy angles (pain, desire, proof, offer, objection, founder story), not small
  variations of one ad.
- Supply vertical 9:16 for Stories/Reels and 4:5 or 1:1 for feed. Do not mix vertical and
  feed media in one ad set when placements are restricted.
- Carousel: 2–10 cards, each with its own media, link and headline.
- Running a Page post as an ad keeps its likes and comments (social proof).
- Advantage+ creative enhancements: decide per account; turn off generative features
  when the brand needs exact wording or imagery.

## Copy fields

Primary text, headline, description, call to action, destination URL, URL parameters,
display link.

- Up to 5 primary texts, 5 headlines and 5 descriptions per ad: Meta mixes them and shows
  the best combination. Not for instant form (lead) ads.
- The destination URL is required on every ad, except ads that open WhatsApp ("Send
  WhatsApp message").
- **URL parameters (UTMs)** on every ad. A common template:
  `utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign={{campaign.name}}&utm_content={{ad.name}}&utm_term={{adset.name}}`.
  Without them, sales cannot be attributed to campaigns outside Meta's own reporting.
- When duplicating ads, check that copy and UTMs came along; new creatives start empty.

## Writing variations

- Base them on what converts in the account: read the best ads' copy first.
- Keep the offer and claims the user gave you. Never invent prices, discounts, guarantees,
  testimonials or results.
- Present the variations for approval before anyone builds them.
- Respect Meta's advertising policies: no personal attributes ("Are you overweight?"), no
  before/after claims in restricted categories, special ad categories for credit,
  employment, housing and social issues.

## Instant forms (lead ads)

The form belongs to the Facebook Page. Create it in Ads Manager or on the Page; a Page
admin must accept Meta's Lead Generation Terms once.
